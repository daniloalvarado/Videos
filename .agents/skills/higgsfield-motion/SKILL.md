# Higgsfield Video Motion Skill (`higgsfield-motion`)

## Core Concept
Combines editorial motion graphics, kinetic text animation, dynamic camera presets (orbit, dramatic push, floating drift), and high-retention visual hooks to create impactful technical and product videos.

---

## 1. Motion Design Principles
1. **Kinetic Typography:** Subtitles and key metrics don't just appear—they punch in with weight, highlight critical keywords dynamically (emerald for positive/control, crimson for risk/deviation, electric blue for framework), and emit subtle audio haptics.
2. **Camera Presets:**
   * **Dramatic Push:** Slow scale zoom-in (1.0 -> 1.08) during high-stakes explanations.
   * **Parallax Drift:** Subtle background pan counter-directional to foreground cards.
   * **Macro Focus:** Camera pans and centers tightly onto a specific metric or audit finding.
3. **Auditory Anchoring:** Every visual change is anchored to a sound event (whoosh on slide, click on badge pop, alert tone on non-conformity).
